Chromahora Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version:

  • Chromahora has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and the developer never receives any data about you.
  • To work out sun times, weather and your town’s name, it sends an approximate location, rounded to about 11 km (7 miles), to sunrise-sunset.org, Apple Weather and Apple Maps. Place searches go to Apple Maps without your location.
  • Everything the app keeps stays on your device, and deleting the app removes it.

Who we are

Chromahora is an iOS app by Aidan Follestad (“we”, “us”). It shows photographers when blue hour and golden hour happen on a given day. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Location

Chromahora asks for permission to use your location only while you’re using the app, and asks for approximate location by default. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Chromahora.

  • Rounded before it’s used. Whether iOS gives the app an approximate or a precise location, Chromahora rounds it to the nearest tenth of a degree, about 11 km (7 miles), before storing it or sending it anywhere. Your precise location never leaves your device.
  • Without permission. If you don’t allow location access, Chromahora estimates your area from your device’s time zone setting instead, using a reference city for that zone that is built into the app. This involves no location access at all.
  • Places you choose. You can also search for another place and see its sun times and weather instead. Its location is rounded the same way before it’s stored or sent anywhere.
  • Never in the background. The app doesn’t track your location over time. It only checks where you are when you open it or come back to it.

Services the app contacts

To show sun times, weather and place names, Chromahora sends requests directly from your device to the three services below, over encrypted connections. We don’t run any servers, so none of this passes through us.

ServiceWhat it’s sentWhy
sunrise-sunset.org The rounded location of your area or the place you chose, a range of dates, and that place’s time zone Sunrise, sunset, golden hour and blue hour times
Apple Weather (WeatherKit) The same rounded location and a range of dates Hourly forecasts of clouds, fog, rain and snow
Apple Maps What you type when you search for a place, without your location, and your rounded location to look up its town Place search, and your town’s name in the title

Like any website or online service, these services can see your device’s IP address when the app contacts them. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle requests: see Apple’s Privacy Policy for Apple Weather and Apple Maps, and sunrise-sunset.org for theirs. None of them receives your name, contact details, or any identifier from the app.

What’s stored on your device

Chromahora keeps a small amount of data on your device so it can open quickly and work offline:

  • Your last rounded location and the time zone it was found in, so the app can show your area at once the next time you open it.
  • Your town’s name, so the app can name your area even offline.
  • Your five most recent places, rounded like your own location, so you can switch back to them.
  • Sun times for the months you’ve viewed, for each place. Months older than last month are deleted automatically.
  • The latest weather forecast for your area and each recent place, which replaces that place’s previous one each time the app fetches a new one.

This data isn’t synced, backed up to our servers, or shared with anyone. Deleting Chromahora removes all of it.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect, receive or store any of your data on our own servers.
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and the app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we don’t sell or share your information.
  • The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or other contact details.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple controls that sharing, and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Chromahora isn’t directed at children, and it doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Open source

Chromahora’s source code is public on GitHub, so you can check for yourself exactly what the app does with your data.

Changes to this policy

If the way Chromahora handles data changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
